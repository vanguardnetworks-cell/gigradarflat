GigRadar Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 5, 2026

GigRadar helps gig drivers check offers against their own rules. This policy explains what information the app uses and where it goes.

Information stored on your device

The rules you set (such as minimum payout and maximum miles), the offers you enter, your blocked restaurants and zones, your history of accepted or passed offers, and your mileage log are saved only on your own device, in the app's local storage. We do not receive this information and we cannot see it.

Information we collect

This version of GigRadar does not have accounts. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or location, and we do not collect them. The app does not use advertising or analytics tools, and it does not track you across other apps or websites.

Hosting

The app is delivered through GitHub Pages. When the app loads, the hosting service may receive technical information such as your IP address and browser type, as it does for any website. We do not control or use that information. GitHub's own privacy statement describes how it handles it.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your information, because we do not collect it.

Deleting your data

To remove everything GigRadar has stored, clear the app's data in your phone's settings or uninstall the app.

Children

GigRadar is intended for adults who do gig work. It is not directed to children.

Estimates

Fuel costs and earnings shown in GigRadar are estimates based on the numbers you enter. They are not financial or tax advice.

Changes to this policy

If we add features that collect information, such as accounts or paid memberships, we will update this policy before they launch and change the effective date above.

Contact
